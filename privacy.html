Privacy Policy

Nashville Chart Book · Effective October 10, 2026

Nashville Chart Book (the "App") is available at number-chart.masterbus.app and is operated by Masterbus ("we", "us"). The App turns an audio recording into a Nashville Number System chart. This policy explains what information the App handles and why.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and password when you create an account. Passwords are handled by our login provider (Supabase) and we never see them in readable form.
  • Audio files you upload. When you create a chart, your audio file is sent to Music.ai, a third-party service that analyzes it for key, tempo, chords and song structure. We do not keep a copy of your audio on our own servers.
  • Your charts. The title, key, tempo, writer/artist name and chord data of charts you create or edit. These are stored in your own Google Drive (if you connect it) or in your browser's local storage on your device. We do not keep a copy of your charts on our servers.
  • Subscription information. Your plan, how many charts you have used this period, whether you have used your free trial, and your Stripe customer and subscription IDs. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Optional Music.ai key. If you choose to use your own Music.ai API key, it is stored only in your browser's local storage and is sent only to Music.ai.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider (Netlify) and database provider (Supabase) may log technical data such as IP address, browser type and request times for security and reliability.

2. Google Drive access

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you connect it, the App asks for a single permission, drive.appdata. This gives the App access only to a hidden application-data folder that the App itself creates in your Drive. The App cannot see, read, or change any of your other Drive files. We use that folder only to save and load your charts so they follow you between devices.

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not let people read it except as needed to provide the App's features, for security, or to comply with the law.

Your Google access token is kept in your browser's local storage and expires after about an hour. You can disconnect at any time by signing out of Google in the App, or by removing the App at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

3. How we use information

  • To create and sign you in to your account.
  • To analyze your audio and produce your chart.
  • To save your charts to your Drive or device.
  • To manage your subscription, track chart usage, and process payments.
  • To keep the App secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers needed to run the App:

  • Supabase – login and account/subscription records.
  • Netlify – hosting and the server functions that run the App.
  • Music.ai – receives the audio you upload so it can be analyzed.
  • Stripe – payments and billing.
  • Google – Drive storage, if you connect it.

Each of these providers handles data under its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Storage on your device

The App uses your browser's local storage to remember charts saved on your device, your optional Music.ai key, and your Google connection. Clearing your browser data removes these.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your account and subscription record while your account is open. To delete your account and its subscription record, email us at the address below. Charts in your Google Drive are yours to delete at any time, and charts saved on your device are removed when you clear your browser data. Stripe may keep payment records as required by law.

7. Security

We use reputable providers and encrypted connections, and our server credentials are kept out of the App's public code. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
